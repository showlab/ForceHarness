Plug the USB plug (USB-A, rectangular metal head) into one of the USB ports on the power strip, all the way in.

The plug is attached to a white data cable. The upward-facing side of the power strip has two rectangular USB-A ports; note which of the holes in the middle to plug into, and locate it first; the smaller oval port next to them is Type-C, which is not the target this time, and neither are the strip's power sockets.

At the start the plug is usually already in the hand, but go by this turn's wrist and exterior photos and the gripper width: if it is not in the hand, first find it and grip the white housing behind the metal head firmly, then plug it in. The power strip's position and orientation and the plug's angle in the hand are not preset; go by the current observation.

You may first move to roughly the right position. After contact, use search to search; if the search fails, change the position and search again until it succeeds
Once found, use insert; if insert cannot get it in (the plug is stuck half on the port), correct it in place with wiggle, pressing while rocking, and do not raise it away first
